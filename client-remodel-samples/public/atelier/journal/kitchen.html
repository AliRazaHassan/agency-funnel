Before you choose a kitchen, consider how you live.

01

Start with a typical day

Before you look at door styles, think about the daily routines the room needs to hold. Who makes breakfast? Where do the bags, groceries and appliances land? What gets in the way? Write down a few specific moments that your new kitchen should make easier.

02

Make storage a conversation

A beautiful cabinet is useful when it has a clear job. Group the things you use together and think about where they belong. A starting inventory helps turn “more storage” into a practical design brief.

03

Decide what matters most

Choose your three biggest priorities. More room to gather, a clearer preparation area or less visible clutter might matter more than adding every feature. These priorities help keep the design focused.

04

Bring your ideas into a brief

Save a few references and explain what you like about each. Colour, material, atmosphere and layout are different clues. A useful brief describes the feeling you want and the problems you want to solve.
