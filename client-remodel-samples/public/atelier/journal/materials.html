← Field notesMATERIALS / 4 MIN READ

Quiet luxury starts with what you can feel.

01

Let one material lead

A palette often feels calmer when one finish carries the character and the others support it. Natural timber, a tactile stone or a soft plaster tone can set the direction.

02

Look beyond the photograph

Photographs communicate atmosphere. Physical samples help you understand texture, finish and colour in your own room. Set them beside one another and view them in changing daylight.

03

Keep everyday life in the picture

Ask about care, maintenance and suitability for the intended use. The right material is a balance between the atmosphere you want and how you will live with it.

04

Leave room for restraint

A room does not need a feature on every surface. A quieter background gives a special detail more presence. Fewer competing finishes can make the overall design feel more resolved.
