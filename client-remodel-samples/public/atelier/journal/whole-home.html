A connected home begins with a consistent idea.

01

Find the thread

Choose a few recurring materials or tones that connect the rooms. Consistency creates a sense of belonging without making every space identical.

02

Consider the transitions

Look at the views between rooms. A doorway, floor transition or line of joinery can have a large effect on how connected the home feels.

03

Give every room a role

Define what each room needs to do and how it should feel. The whole home can share a language while each space has a different character.

04

Think about the sequence

Agree a clear scope and discuss any phasing before the work begins. A cohesive direction helps later decisions belong to the same idea.
