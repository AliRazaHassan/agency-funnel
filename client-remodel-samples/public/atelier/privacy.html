ABOUT YOUR INFORMATION

Your ideas.
Your privacy.

This page explains how this illustrative website preview handles your project information.

Your project brief

The preview stores your project draft and selected design mood in your browser’s local storage. No enquiry is sent. You can remove this information with “Clear my saved draft” on the project enquiry page, or through your browser settings.

Reference photographs

Selected photographs are previewed on your device. They are not uploaded to a server and are not included in the downloaded text brief.

Design companion

In guided preview mode, responses use preset suggestions in your browser. If live AI is connected later, a message you submit and recent conversation context are sent to the server and the configured AI provider. Do not include sensitive personal information in your design questions.

Website hosting

The hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to serve the website. This preview does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Before a client launch

The actual business’s contact details and policies will replace this preview notice when its enquiry delivery, analytics and business content are configured.
