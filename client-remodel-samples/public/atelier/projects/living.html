THE IDEA

One connected idea. Room to breathe.

A whole-home concept built around natural light, tactile materials and a calmer relationship between living spaces. The room offers a cohesive direction rather than a measured construction proposal.

THREE CONSIDERED MOVES
01

Space, rather than stuff

A restrained furniture plan gives each piece room and lets the architecture do more of the work.

02

A natural connection

Timber, limestone and soft linen create continuity between surfaces and everyday objects.

03

Light as a material

A connection to the courtyard brings daylight, greenery and a gentle sense of movement into the room.
