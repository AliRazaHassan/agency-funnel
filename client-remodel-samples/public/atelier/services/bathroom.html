A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Where should I start?

Start with what isn't working and what you want the room to feel like. Share your priorities, a few photos and any known constraints.

How is the scope agreed?

The layout, materials, project conditions and desired finish all shape the scope. A site-specific review and written specification are needed before a construction commitment.

Can I keep my existing layout?

Often the design exploration can begin with the existing layout. What can stay or change needs to be confirmed after your space and requirements are reviewed.
