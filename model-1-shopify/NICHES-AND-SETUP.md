# Model 1 — Shopify Turnkey Stores

## Top 3 high-demand niches (start here)

1. **Pet Supplies** — repeat buys, strong Google + TikTok demand, easy supplier catalogs.
2. **Home Fitness Gear** — bands, mats, recovery tools; high AOV bundles.
3. **Eco / Kitchen Gadgets** — reusable + small appliances; gift-friendly margins.

Avoid overcrowded fashion/dropship meme niches for first 3 stores.

## Pre-build checklist (per niche)

- [ ] Shopify Partner account → Development store
- [ ] Theme: clean premium theme (Dawn customized or paid minimal theme)
- [ ] AutoDS or Zendrop connected + 20–40 products imported
- [ ] Matrixify ready for bulk titles/tags/SEO
- [ ] Shopify Flow: order → supplier, refund alert, low-stock (if available)
- [ ] Pages: Shipping, Tracking, Returns, About, Contact
- [ ] AI-written product descriptions (unique per SKU, not supplier paste)
- [ ] Payment + shipping zones configured (demo-ready)
- [ ] Brand: logo, colors, domain placeholder ready to transfer

## Pricing guide (transfer-ready store)

- Basic automated store: **$300–$500**
- Premium (brand + email/WhatsApp flows stub + 40+ products): **$600–$800**

## Pitch line

"You don't wait 30 days. Ownership transfers today — store is live-ready."

## Week-1 target

Build **one** Pet Supplies store end-to-end as the sales demo.
